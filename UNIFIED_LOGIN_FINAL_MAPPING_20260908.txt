Selection behavior
- Login once with email/password.
- Choose one active role belonging to the email.
- Choose one system allowed for that role.
- Clicking the selected role/system again unselects it.
- Clear selection is available on both screens.
- Change role is available from the system screen.

Observer rule
Observer and External Observer are Teacher accounts. These roles only appear when an active observer/external_observer row exists for that teacherID.
